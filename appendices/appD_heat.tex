\chapter{Heat kernels and spectral asymptotics}\label{app:heat}
For a Laplace-type operator $P=-(g^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu+E)$ on a compact manifold of dimension $n$, the heat kernel trace has the asymptotic expansion $\Tr\ee^{-tP}\sim(4\pi t)^{-n/2}\sum_{k\ge0}t^{k}a_{2k}(P)$ with local invariants $a_0=\int\sqrt g\,\mathrm{tr}\,\id$, $a_2=\int\sqrt g\,\mathrm{tr}(\tfrac R6+E)$, and $a_4$ quadratic in curvature (the Seeley--DeWitt coefficients). The spectral action \cref{ch:spectral_action} follows by writing $f(D^2/\Lambda^2)$ via Laplace transform: the moments of $f$ multiply the $a_{2k}$. For the Dirac operator $D^2=\nabla^*\nabla+R/4$ (Lichnerowicz), so $E=-R/4$ and $\tfrac R6+E=-\tfrac R{12}$ per spinor component. The coefficients are standard (Vassilevich's heat-kernel review); we do not reproduce $a_4$.
