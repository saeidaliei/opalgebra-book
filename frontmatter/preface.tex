\chapter*{Preface}
This book is an introduction to operator algebras, modular theory and their recent role in quantum gravity, written for physicists with a graduate-level background and no formal analysis. The goal is to make the following sentence precise and checkable: \emph{in quantum field theory the algebra of a region is of type III$_1$ and has no entropy; gravity, by turning the modular Hamiltonian into a constraint and forcing the inclusion of an observer, replaces it by a type II algebra with a trace, and in de Sitter space by a type II$_1$ algebra with a maximum-entropy state}. Parts I--V build the mathematics with physical motivation first, Part VI supplies algebraic QFT, Part VII is the main physics payoff (de Sitter and observers), Part VIII sketches black holes and holography, and Part IX is an independent intermezzo on Connes' noncommutative geometry.

\textbf{Sources and checks.} The mathematical chapters (1--7) are developed from standard references and were cross-checked, including a numerical check of the finite-dimensional crossed-product entropy (\texttt{scripts/check\_toy.py}). The chapters on recent research (8--12) follow the cited papers; Chapter~\ref{ch:clpw_ch} were checked line by line against the de Sitter paper of Chandrasekaran--Longo--Penington--Witten, with the entropy formula for a general state tested numerically (\texttt{scripts/check\_clpw\_general.py}). Bibliographic entries were checked against journal records wherever the \texttt{note} field of \texttt{refs.bib} says ``verified''; the remaining entries are standard citations whose details were not re-checked. Exercises were re-derived by the author; Appendix~\ref{app:solutions} gives a solution to every exercise. Corrections are welcome.
