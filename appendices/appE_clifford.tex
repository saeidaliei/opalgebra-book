\chapter{Clifford algebras and spinors}\label{app:clifford}
The Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}_n$ is generated by $\gamma^\mu$ with $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2\delta^{\mu\nu}$. In even dimension $n=2m$ it is isomorphic to $M_{2^m}(\C)$, with unique irreducible representation on spinors $\C^{2^m}$; the chirality element $\gamma_5$ gives a $\Z_2$ grading. The Dirac operator on a spin manifold is $D=\ii\gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu$, self-adjoint on $L^2(M,S)$ with discrete spectrum for compact $M$. The charge conjugation $J$ and its signs depend on the dimension modulo $8$ (KO-dimension), important for \cref{ch:almost_comm}.
