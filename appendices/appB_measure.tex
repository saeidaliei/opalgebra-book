\chapter{Measure theory and distributions}\label{app:measure}
\textbf{Measures.} A $\sigma$-algebra of subsets, a countably additive $\mu\ge0$. $\Linf(X,\mu)$ is the commutative von Neumann algebra of essentially bounded functions on $L^2(X,\mu)$. \textbf{Radon--Nikodym.} If $\nu\ll\mu$ there is $f=\dd\nu/\dd\mu$ with $\nu(E)=\int_Ef\dd\mu$; the algebraic analogue is $\rho_\omega=\dd\omega/\dd\tau$ (\cref{ch:traces}). \textbf{Projection-valued measures} (\cref{ch:spectral}) generalize measures to operator values. \textbf{Distributions.} A distribution is a continuous linear functional on test functions; quantum fields are operator-valued distributions $\phi(f)$ (\cref{ch:haagkastler}); the product of distributions at a point is ill-defined, which is one reason for algebras of bounded smeared operators. The Fourier transform diagonalizes translations: $\delta(x)=\int\frac{\dd k}{2\pi}\ee^{\ii kx}$ is used in group averaging (\cref{ch:groups}).
