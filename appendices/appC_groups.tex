\chapter{Groups, Haar measure, Lie algebras}\label{app:groups}
A locally compact group $G$ has a left Haar measure, unique up to scale; for abelian groups ($\R$, $U(1)$) and compact groups it is two-sided. For $G=\R$ it is $\dd t$; for $U(1)$ it is $\dd\theta/2\pi$. \textbf{Characters and Fourier duality:} the dual of $\R$ is $\R$, of $U(1)$ is $\Z$, of $\Z$ is $U(1)$; crossed products by $\R$ come with a dual action of $\R$ (\cref{ch:takesaki}). \textbf{Lie algebras.} For a Lie group, one-parameter subgroups $\ee^{tX}$ are generated by Lie algebra elements $X$; unitary representations give self-adjoint generators (Stone, \cref{ch:spectral}). \textbf{Poincar\'e and de Sitter groups:} $\mathrm{ISO}(1,d)$ and $SO(1,d+1)$; the boost generator $B$ in the plane $(x^0,x^1)$ has spectrum $\R$ in unitary positive-energy representations.
