\chapter*{Preface}
This book is an introduction to operator algebras, modular theory and their recent role in quantum gravity, written for physicists with a graduate-level background and no formal analysis. The goal is to make the following sentence precise and checkable: \emph{in quantum field theory the algebra of a region is of type III$_1$ and has no entropy; gravity, by turning the modular Hamiltonian into a constraint and forcing the inclusion of an observer, replaces it by a type II algebra with a trace, and in de Sitter space by a type II$_1$ algebra with a maximum-entropy state}. Parts I--V build the mathematics with physical motivation first, Part VI supplies algebraic QFT, Part VII is the main physics payoff (de Sitter and observers), Part VIII sketches black holes and holography, and Part IX is an independent intermezzo on Connes' noncommutative geometry.

\textbf{Status of this draft.} The mathematical chapters (1--29) were written and cross-checked carefully, including a numerical check of the finite-dimensional crossed-product entropy (\texttt{scripts/check\_toy.py}). The chapters on recent research (30--44, 49--52) follow the structure and abstracts of the original papers; those entries marked ``verified'' in the bibliography were checked, the rest were not. Chapters flagged ``Status of the literature'' are particularly provisional. Exercises have been checked by the author but not independently. Please treat this as a draft for review, not a finished text.
