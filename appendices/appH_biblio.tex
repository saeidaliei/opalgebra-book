\section*{Notes on sources and checks}
The mathematical chapters (1--7) are developed from the standard references listed in the bibliography (Bratteli--Robinson, Takesaki, Kadison--Ringrose, Reed--Simon, Haag, Connes). The finite-dimensional crossed-product entropy of \cref{ch:crossed_products} was cross-checked numerically. The chapters on recent research (8--12) follow the cited papers; \cref{ch:clpw_ch} was checked line by line against the de Sitter paper of Chandrasekaran, Longo, Penington and Witten, and its entropy formula for a general state was tested numerically. The numerical checks are available in the source repository accompanying the book. Every exercise was re-derived, and \cref{app:solutions} gives a solution to each one. Bibliographic details of the recent papers were checked against arXiv and journal records.
